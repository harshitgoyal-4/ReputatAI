Questions You Might Have

Everything you need to know about integrating ReputatAI into your brand's operations.

How does ReputatAI connect to our review channels and communities?
+
ReputatAI connects via official platform APIs, public webhook feeds, and read-only connectors for Trustpilot, Reddit, Google Reviews, Apple App Store, and Google Play. Integration takes less than 15 minutes with zero code modifications to your existing application.
Can our human support team review and approve AI responses before publishing?
+
Yes, absolutely. ReputatAI supports two operational modes: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) where AI de-escalations are staged as one-click draft approvals for your support agents, and Autonomous Sentinel mode for low-risk standard review de-escalations with strict guardrails.
How does ReputatAI avoid sounding like a generic corporate bot?
+
Our multi-LLM engine ingests your brand's specific tone guidelines, previous high-rated support interactions, and customer vocabulary. Each response acknowledges the user's specific context rather than using static canned templates.
How does competitor benchmarking work?
+
We continuously benchmark public sentiment, response velocity, and recurring complaint topics across your selected peer competitors. This provides executive visibility into where competitors are losing customer trust and where your platform holds a market advantage.
Can you add custom sites, niche forums, or private channels on request?
+
Yes, 100%. Our ingestion engine is fully modular. While we support all major platforms out of the box (Trustpilot, Reddit, Google Reviews, App Stores, X), we regularly build bespoke connectors. If your brand needs custom monitoring for Sitejabber, Bitcointalk, Discord channels, Telegram groups, or proprietary community forums, our team engineers and connects custom add-ons within 48 hours.
How fast can we launch a confidential pilot?
+
We can deploy a zero-risk 14-day confidential audit and active defense pilot within 48 hours. No long-term commitments or setup fees are required.
